# Debian maintenance routing proposal

This is a proposal, not team consent. No team is marked as `Maintainer` or
`Uploaders` until a maintainer confirms the current team policy and accepts
co-maintenance.

| Source package | Candidate route | Contact/policy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gmpfrxx-mkii` | Debian Science, or individual maintainer with Science co-maintenance | [Debian Science](https://wiki.debian.org/Teams/DebianScience) | PROPOSED |
| `mplapack` | Debian Science | [Debian Science](https://wiki.debian.org/Teams/DebianScience) | PROPOSED |
| `octave-mplapack-interop` | Debian Octave Group first; Debian Science may co-maintain the dependency stack | [Debian Octave Group](https://wiki.debian.org/Teams/DebianOctaveGroup) | PROPOSED |

The initial package names are also proposals. Before an ITP or Salsa import,
the maintainer must check the current team policy, request consent, and select
the correct Debian `Maintainer`/`Uploaders` fields. The contact routes are
recorded here so that P02–P04 do not silently invent team ownership.
